# Budget planning

Follow the [budget workflow](../../../docs/workflows/budget-planning.md) and adapt the [budget template](../../../docs/templates/budget.template.md) to confirmed project facts. Identify the billing account, currency, period, authorized spending and existing subscriptions before estimating. Never request credentials or copy private billing details into the repository.

Inventory subscription and metered costs, including coding and reviewer agents, retries, model tokens and tools, CI, hosting, data, storage, traffic, domains, and services such as ChatGPT Sites. Verify current rates, included allowances and account-specific terms from authoritative sources; record the source and checked date. Mark missing rates or usage pending rather than zero. Distinguish attributable cost, included or prepaid allowance, and incremental cash cost without counting one shared subscription twice.

Produce low, expected and high scenarios with explicit usage assumptions and units, then compare actual usage with the forecast. Stay within the user's existing spending authorization. Before optional work that would exceed it, present the concrete cost and obtain authorization; do not repeatedly ask for routine usage already authorized. Distinguish budget alerts from enforced limits and do not claim this document limits provider billing.
